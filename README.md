# Manufacturing Quality & Operations Analytics | Power BI

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze manufacturing production, quality, cost, and operational efficiency.

The project provides an executive-level overview of manufacturing performance and a detailed operational analysis to help identify patterns across production cost, quality, defects, downtime, delivery delays, inventory turnover, safety incidents, and energy consumption.

---

## 📊 Project Overview

Manufacturing organizations need to monitor multiple operational metrics simultaneously to understand production performance, quality outcomes, and operational efficiency.

This project transforms manufacturing data into an interactive two-page Power BI dashboard that enables users to:

- Monitor overall production performance
- Analyze production costs
- Evaluate product and supplier quality
- Understand defect patterns
- Analyze downtime and delivery delays
- Monitor inventory turnover
- Track safety incidents
- Evaluate energy consumption and efficiency
- Compare operational performance across different categories

The dashboard is designed to provide both **high-level business visibility** and **detailed operational analysis**.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are to:

1. Monitor overall manufacturing production performance.
2. Analyze production costs across different categories.
3. Evaluate quality and supplier performance.
4. Identify defect distribution and defect-related patterns.
5. Analyze downtime and delivery delays.
6. Monitor inventory turnover and operational efficiency.
7. Track safety incidents.
8. Analyze energy consumption and energy efficiency.
9. Provide interactive filtering for business users.
10. Present complex manufacturing data through an easy-to-understand visual dashboard.

---

## 📑 Dashboard Structure

The Power BI report contains two analytical pages.

### 1. Executive Performance Dashboard

Provides a high-level overview of manufacturing quality and operational performance.

#### Key KPIs

- Total Production Volume
- Total Production Cost
- Average Quality Score
- Average Supplier Quality
- Average Defect Rate
- High Defect %

#### Visual Analysis

- Quality Level Distribution
- Average Production Cost by Quality Level
- Defect Distribution
- Production Volume by Quality Level

#### Interactive Filters

- Production Cost
- Quality Level
- Defect Level

---

### 2. Production & Efficiency Dashboard

Provides a more detailed view of production efficiency and operational performance.

#### Key KPIs

- Production Volume
- Average Downtime %
- Average Delivery Delay
- Average Inventory Turnover
- Safety Incidents
- Average Energy Consumption

#### Visual Analysis

- Average Downtime by Cost Level
- Average Delivery Delay by Cost Level
- Average Energy Efficiency by Cost Level
- Safety Incidents by Cost Level

#### Interactive Filters

- Production Cost
- Defect Category
- Quality Category
